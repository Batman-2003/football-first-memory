My First Football Experience

Abayomi Oluwatobiloba

My first experience with football happened in 2009, a couple of months before I turned 6 years old. There was the game called 'Manchester United Club Football' I downloaded from sefan.ru — it featured players like Wayne Rooney, Cristiano Ronaldo, Paul Scholes, and Ryan Giggs. That single game lit the spark.

I'd often play with my classmates in primary school, using plastic bottles — BOBO, etc. — as our ball. The dirt, the laughter, the makeshift goals … it became more than a hobby for me. It became a piece of my identity.

“In 2010, during the FIFA WORLD CUP: SOUTH AFRICA, I realised one of the names I saw on the games had moved to a club called REAL MADRID CLUB DE FOOTBALL — and that was how I became a Real Madrid fan.”

I started following matches and watching highlights before heading to school in the morning, while waiting for the school bus. Back then, I didn't fully understand tactics or player names — but I didn't need to. I just knew Real Madrid was a club I would love to support for a long, long time.

From plastic bottles to Champions League nights — football taught me loyalty, joy, and that some loves start with a single click on a game. Hala Madrid. 🤍💚
